Since you are submitting this to the **ATScience x IOSP** track, the key is to frame SpaceTimeArc not just as a "GIS project," but as a **domain-specific implementation of the decentralized science (DeSci) stack**. 

The reviewers will be looking for how you use (or intend to use) the "substrate" (ATProto, IPFS, MIRA, Matadisco) to solve real-world research problems.

Depending on whether you want a deeper dive (Talk) or a quick demo (Lightning Talk), I have prepared two versions.

### Option 1: Regular Talk (25 min)

*Best for showcasing the full System Architecture and the "Integrated 4D Landscapes" vision.*

**Track:** Compose / Share / Sustain
**Proposed Title:** **Time-Traveling the Earth: Building a Decentralized 4D Landscape Infrastructure with SpaceTimeArc**

**Abstract:**
How do we build a scientific record of the Earth's surface that is as dynamic as the planet itself, spanning from 200 million years ago to the year 2100? SpaceTimeArc is a data infrastructure designed for spatio-temporal human-environment interaction modelling—a "virtual globe with a timeslider." 

While the vision is geovisualization, the core challenge is the discovery and preservation of heterogeneous, multi-temporal environmental and cultural datasets. In this talk, we present the SpaceTimeArc System Architecture: a three-tier approach integrating Cloud-Native Geospatial (CNG) formats with a decentralized discovery layer. We will demonstrate how the project leverages Matadisco and AT Protocol to solve the "URL management problem" for large-scale COGs (Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs), moving away from siloed repositories toward a researcher-owned discovery network. We will also discuss the alignment of our spatio-temporal data model with the MIRA schema, proposing a path toward granular, attributable "modular science" for the geosciences. By combining CIDOC CRM-based semantics with a distributed substrate, SpaceTimeArc aims to turn 4D landscapes into a shared, verifiable scientific record.

### Option 2: Lightning Talk (10 min)

*Best for a high-impact "teaser" focused on the technical implementation of Matadisco/CNG.*

**Track:** Share / Verify
**Proposed Title:** **Decentralizing the Globe: Matadisco and Cloud-Native Geospatial Data**

**Abstract:**
Finding the right spatiotemporal dataset often means navigating dozens of siloed portals. SpaceTimeArc is changing this by implementing a decentralized discovery layer for "4D Landscapes." We present a working pipeline that transforms massive Cloud-Native Geospatial (CNG) datasets into signed, discoverable records on the AT Protocol via Matadisco. By separating data storage (S3/IPFS) from discovery (ATProto), we enable a resilient, community-driven index of the Earth's past, present, and future. This lightning talk demonstrates how a "geospatial-native" approach to the open social web can accelerate integrative geography and provide a blueprint for other domain-specific research records to move from silos to the substrate.
